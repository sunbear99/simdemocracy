🗳️ How Voting Works

SimDemocracy uses a secure, anonymous, cookie-based voting system. This ensures that while we know that you voted (preventing double-votes), we do not track what you voted for.

🟢 "Vote Cast" vs 🟡 "Edit Vote"

🟡 Edit Vote

Meaning: You have full access.

We found the secure token on your device. You can simply click the button to change your vote.

🟢 Vote Cast

Meaning: Your vote is counted, but locked.

We know you voted, but this device does not have the key to unlock your ballot (e.g., you are on a different device or cleared your cookies).

🔑 How to Recover a Locked Vote

If you see the green Vote Cast button but want to change your vote, you have two options:

  1. Switch Devices: Return to the device (phone/laptop) where you originally voted. The "Edit Vote" button should appear there automatically.
  2. Manual Token Entry (New!):

    If you saved your 12-character Verification Token when you first voted:

    • Click the green ✅ Vote Cast button on the dashboard.
    • You will be taken to a "Vote Locked" page.
    • Paste your token into the box and click Unlock.
    • This will restore your session and allow you to edit your ballot.
